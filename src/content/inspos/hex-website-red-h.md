### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Collect scanned paper cut-outs: letters, small figures, buildings, an eye, a card shape, with a few hand-drawn lines on top. Keep the scan edges and the slight roughness.
2. Put them on near-black and animate on twos or threes, at about 10fps. Move or swap one thing per frame and don't ease anything.
3. Let one red letter stand in for the name and spell it out over a few frames (H, then E, then X, then HEX).
4. Cut between scenes every 1-2 seconds. Use a large blue photographic scene as the big contrast to the black ones.
5. Finish on a figure walking over a loading bar.
