### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Light a dark figure from one side so most of the frame falls to black.
2. Draw a halo of a few hundred thin bars radiating from a point behind the head, each with its own random length.
3. Re-roll the lengths on every frame (or every second frame) so the halo flickers, and shift a cool and a warm tint back and forth across the bars.
4. Keep the figure locked so only the halo moves, and add grain over the whole image.
