### What happens
Rows of white capitals on black type themselves in, fill with colour blocks, then delete, on a loop of about 2.5s (four passes in the clip).

- 0.0-0.4s: single coloured asterisk blocks appear down the left, stepping diagonally. A few letters (A, ANY) appear before them.
- 0.4-1.0s: nine rows type in at once, left to right, each with an asterisk block trailing the text like a cursor. Rows are indented in a chevron: each row sits further right than the one above until the middle row, then back left.
- 1.0-1.6s: each row grows from one line to four lines of text: "ANY TOKEN * ANY CHAIN * ONE ENDPOINT / LIVE PRICES, DEEP HISTORY, NO GAPS * / 92M TOKENS * 8 NETWORKS * UNDER 200MS / QUERY ONCE * KNOW THE MARKET". The rows overlap and stack into a dense slanted block.
- Throughout the typing, single words get solid colour blocks behind them (green, pink, cyan, blue, red, yellow, white). The colours keep changing while the text appears.
- 1.8-2.2s: text deletes, with the left side vanishing first and fragments such as "E MARKET" left on the right, with only the asterisk blocks remaining.
- about 2.4s: nearly empty, one or two asterisk blocks. The cycle then restarts.

### Timing and feel
A 2.5s loop: about 1.2s typing, about 0.3s hold, about 0.6s deleting, short blank. Steps are per character, linear. Colour blocks change on a stepped beat rather than fading. No interaction.

### What it is made of
Black ground (#000), bold grotesque caps in white at one size with tight leading. Highlight colours are flat and saturated (about green #20f020, magenta #e050e0, cyan #20e8d0, blue #2020ff, red #e01010, yellow #f0f020, white). Blocks fit the word width, and text on them is black on bright colours, white on blue and red. Numbers like 92M and 200MS look lighter and monospaced. Asterisk glyphs separate phrases and also sit in colour blocks as end markers.

### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
- Set a block of caps in one tight, bold grotesque.
- Repeat the block nine times, with indents that step right then back left.
- Type all rows together, character by character, and add random flat colour blocks behind words and a marker at the line end.
- Reverse the effect by deleting from the left, and restart after a beat. About 2.5s per pass.
