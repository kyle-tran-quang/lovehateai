### What happens
Video A, the portfolio (30s). I sampled once a second, so I can describe order and rough timing but not the fine motion between samples, except for 1 to 5.5s where I looked four times a second.

- 0.0 to 1.5s: a wall of grey CRT monitors in rows on black, each screen showing a different graphic (a green triangle over stacked layers, a blue screen with fine lines and the words "Foundational AI", a white screen with a pale mark, a cloth-like image, a red triangle with a blue cube). The monitors at the left and right edges are turned at angles, so the wall seems to curve or sit in perspective. A cursor arrow is visible.
- About 1.75 to 2.0s: the view pushes quickly in on one monitor until it fills the frame. The others fall into dim shadows around it. Its screen shows a burst of thin glowing lines with dots at the ends.
- 2.0 to 4.0s: the lines on that screen keep their shape but their colour slides from red to orange, yellow, green and then to blue and violet, so it ends up rainbow. The monitor itself holds still.
- About 4.0s: cut into a project view on black. Grey text ("t-shirts,"), a white line drawing of a box corner, and round white sticker tags tilted at different angles ("VERCEL", "ILLUSTRATION", "2025"). A white round button top right carries a circular arrow, then turns into an X.
- 5 to 7s: more stickers and objects float in and out at various tilts: a red Etch A Sketch with a gem, a wireframe tile, "SKETCH", "STICKER", "SKETCH STICKERS" tags. The top-right button reverts to an arrow, as the tags shrink away toward it.

### Timing and feel
In A, the push-in on the monitor is fast (about a quarter of a second) and ends on a soft stop. The colour shift on the screen is slow, about 2s. Object swaps from 4s on feel like a cut or short slide between full-frame items, each held for 2 to 3s, with a slight tilt, as though in 3D space. The pace is calm at first and then busy near the end. A cursor shows up on most frames and moves a little, and the round button top right looks like a back or close control, so the piece is probably interactive, but I can't see a click happening. In B, each role stays about 1.2 to 1.7s. The swap is an ease-out looking vertical slide with a short letter glitch. It plays once through in this clip.

### What it is made of
A: black ground with a soft lighter band at the bottom. Glossy rendered or photographic objects: pale-grey plastic CRT monitors, app icons, round badges, tilted card-like tiles. Flat graphics on the monitor screens. White sticker pills with an all-caps mono or grotesque label. Whether the 3D objects are rendered, modelled or photographed is not clear from the frames. B: a light grotesque for the name, a slightly softer grey for roles, with a few letters swapped to other faces.

### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Start with one dark space and a wall of the same object (here, monitors), each showing a different piece of work. Let the edges fall away in perspective.
2. Push the camera into one object quickly, dim the rest, and let that screen show its piece. Slide its colours through the spectrum for about two seconds.
3. Treat each project as a set of loose, slightly tilted objects (stickers, icons, cards) that come to the centre one by one.
4. Keep one round control in the top right corner for going back, and change its icon between arrow and X.
5. For the header, use one line with a fixed name and a rotating role. Swap roles with a short vertical slide, and at each swap turn a few letters into a different face for a beat.
