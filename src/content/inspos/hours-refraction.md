### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Black page, one short headline, one small pill button, and a single row of heavy 3D marks resting near the bottom.
2. Start the marks in near-black, glassy form, and bring them to bright cyan one at a time over about 1.5s, left to right, with a short delay each.
3. Give them thick, rounded bevels and let a lighter rim show where one shape passes in front of another, so they look like thick glass.

5. Let the button fill white on hover and dim briefly on press.
