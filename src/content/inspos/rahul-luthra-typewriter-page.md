### What happens
A white landing page screen recording. A rounded grey pill nav (App, Explore, Docs, Demo) sits top centre, with a small dark dot and the name "Dreamvision" below it, then the headline "For the love of typing one keystroke at a time." in a light serif, a grey two-line sentence, a dark pill button "Request a demo", and a dark typewriter at the bottom edge with a sheet of paper in it. A cursor arrow sits right of the typewriter and does not move in the frames I checked. The page itself never scrolls.

Everything animated happens on the typewriter's paper:
- About 0 to 8s: text is typed onto the blank sheet, a character at a time, in a small typewriter face. First "Dear reader, slow down." then, on a second line, "Every word counts." The text is tiny at this resolution and I could not see the keys or carriage move.

- About 10.5 to 11.5s: a new blank sheet comes up from behind the roller and stops at the same height as before.
- About 11.5 to 12.6s: blank page, still.

(My timestamps could be off by up to 0.5s because the frames came from a variable-frame-rate file.)

### Timing and feel
Typing is bursty rather than even, about 5 to 8 characters a second, with small pauses that feel like a person. The second run looks a little faster than the first. The paper eject is quick (about half a second) and ease-out looking, and the new sheet feeds in slower, about a second, with a soft stop. It reads as a loop of type, eject, reload, type. Whether the page restarts at the end is not confirmed by the footage. No trigger: no click or scroll is visible, and it seems to run by itself.

### What it is made of
White ground, a light high-contrast serif for the headline (near black, about #1a1a1a), a grey sans paragraph, a pill nav with a light grey fill, a pill button in near black with a small icon. The typewriter is a dark, glossy, rendered-looking object (charcoal, about #2a2f33) with soft highlights on the keys and rollers, cropped at the bottom of the frame. The paper is pale grey fading to white at the top. The typed text is a slab-like mono, in near black.

### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Build a quiet centred landing page: pill nav, serif headline, short grey subline, one dark button.
2. Put one realistic object cropped by the bottom edge, with a sheet of paper in it that can move on its own.
3. Type a short two-line message onto the paper at a human, uneven pace.
4. When it is done, hold it for about a second, slide the sheet up and out with a quick ease-out, then slide a blank one in more slowly and start again.
5. Let the sheet overlap the page content as it leaves, so the object seems to belong to the page.
