A fine grain over everything. Heavy outlines in yellow or white around key objects. No 3D, blur or parallax. The frame is square and the drawing fills it edge to edge.

### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
- Draw each scene in a flat four-colour palette on black, with stipple for shading and a thick coloured outline round the main object.
- Keep one idea per scene (eye, desk, forest, shelf) and change the scene every half second or so.
- Let objects from one scene sit in the same place as objects in the next (an eye stays an eye, a beam stays a beam) so it reads as a morph.
- Reveal detail in steps: add animals or stars a few at a time across several frames.
- Use a few radiating lines as the one big gesture, once, around the midpoint.
- Add a light grain at the end, not a blur or glow.
