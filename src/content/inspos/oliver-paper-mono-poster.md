### What it is made of
A monospaced typeface shown in several ways: very large, outlined, tilted, as a numeral set, as a block of running text. Palette is mostly pale blue (about #bcd0f5, approximate), a mid blue, navy (#1e2a5e, approximate), pink (about #f5a0cf) and black, with one white poster. Textures: marbled ink, a fine halftone or pixel grid, and a speckle that looks like photocopy grain. Small registration-style corner marks appear on a few. Everything here is flat 2D artwork.

### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Pick one typeface and one palette (pale blue, navy, pink, a little black).
2. Make about nine posters that each show the type a different way: huge and rotated, outlined, as a numeral grid, as dense text, as a tiny spec sheet.
3. Lay a marbled or grainy texture under some of them, and a pixel grid over a few.
4. Export square and cut them together at about 0.4s each, no transitions.
