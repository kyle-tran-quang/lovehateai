### What happens
The poster's words: "Some micrographics from recent brand work for Strata." Six hard cuts on a half-second beat, alternating black and white. Each card is a small group of type, lines and icons in the middle of the frame. Nothing moves inside a card.

0.0-0.5s black. A label-like block: "STRUCTURE / ID ST-024" and a barcode at top, a stack of coloured lines (red, blue, yellow, light grey) beside four lines of small mono text, three outlined icons, the word "Strata" in heavy sans, and "LAYER / 04" beside "Q4/2026".
0.5-1.0s white. "AI SECURITY / THE WALL" with an arrow line, a globe icon, three short coloured bars next to small text, a hatched band, small icons and the logo, a line of stripes under it.
1.0-1.5s black. A paragraph of mono text with three words highlighted on coloured boxes (red, blue, yellow), a layered-diamond icon, a hatched band, a brick icon and a line of small text.
1.5-2.0s white. A big "Strata", two overlapping circles (yellow and red, with blue lines through the overlap), and a short text block under it.
2.0-2.5s black. The logo with three icons, coloured stripes, an outline drawing of an arch, a paragraph, and two lines of small text at the bottom.
2.5-3.0s white. The logo with a small overlapping circle pair, a half-circle icon, a box with diagonal lines, short text and a stack of stripes.

### Timing and feel
Exactly 0.5s per card, with an abrupt change in one frame, no fades or slides. The switching between black and white gives it a strobe-like rhythm. Within each card the picture is static, with a rare single-frame blip near the cut at about 0.43s. The last card does not return to the first, so I can't say it loops.

### What it is made of
Heavy sans for "Strata" (about white on black, near-black on white), a monospace face for all the small text, thin outline icons with a consistent stroke, hairline rules, hatching, and a four-colour palette used as bars and stripes: red (about #d6404a, approximate), blue (about #4f86b0), yellow (about #e3b240) and a light grey. Layout is centred and takes less than a third of the frame, with a large empty margin.

### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Make a small kit of parts: one heavy wordmark, one mono face for labels, a set of outline icons, stripes and a hatch pattern.
2. Compose six compact "label" cards from it, each roughly a third of the frame, and alternate black and white grounds.
3. Cut between them on a fixed 0.5s beat with no transitions.
4. Keep each card still and reuse the same four colours across all of them.
