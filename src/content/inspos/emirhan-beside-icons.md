0.5-4s: icons pop in one at a time, about two a second. The pull-back looks continuous and smooth (looks like ease-in-out over about 8s). The mark-to-wordmark morph is fast, about 0.8s in total. No interaction, no cursor.

### What it is made of
Black outline icons with thick, slightly wobbly, hand-drawn strokes and rounded ends, on white (#fff). Some icons have solid fills (the calendar block, the bell clapper). The wordmark is a bold geometric sans in black. The group mark is made of tall rounded bars with round heads. No colour, no gradients, no shadow. Motion blur on the wordmark is visible. How the 490 icons were arranged into the group shape can't be told from the footage.

### To recreate the look
Inferred from the footage, untested.
1. Draw icons with a thick uneven line and rotate each a few degrees.
2. Start with one icon centred, then add icons around it on a growing beat, each popping from small to full size.
3. Slowly scale the whole canvas down so the pile always fits, and let the pile's outline shape itself into the brand mark.
4. Thicken the strokes until the icons merge into a single solid black mark.
5. Reveal the wordmark from left to right with a short horizontal blur on each letter, then hold on the logo for a couple of seconds.
